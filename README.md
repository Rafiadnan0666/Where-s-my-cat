# 🐱 Where’s My Cat?

> *"Your cat is missing. The city is big. The clock is ticking."*

**Where’s My Cat?** is a first-person exploration & adventure game built in **Unity** where you roam a sprawling low-poly city in search of your lost feline friend.  
Along the way, you’ll navigate houses, streets, suspicious neighbors, and possibly armed citizens. Yes, the neighborhood is *that* kind of place.

---

## 🎮 The Mission

Your cat has disappeared — maybe slipped out the door, maybe kidnapped by the shady guy next door, maybe hiding in the weird furniture store.  
You’ll have to:
- Search every alley, house, and rooftop.
- Question your life choices when you find *another* stray that isn’t yours.
- Avoid unnecessary confrontations with people carrying WWII rifles.
- Find your cat before… something bad happens.

---

## 🕹️ Features
- **Exploration** – Walk, run, and sneak your way around a detailed city.
- **Interactive World** – Houses, interiors, furniture, and props to investigate.
- **Animal Encounters** – Not just cats… dogs, birds, and maybe raccoons.
- **Atmosphere** – Low-poly style with cozy lighting and city ambience.
- **Multiple Locations** – From small apartments to wide streets.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
- **Engine:** Unity
- **Languages:** C#, ShaderLab, HLSL
- **Assets:**
  - SimplePoly City – Low Poly Assets
  - Furniture_FREE
  - StylizedGuns - WW2 US
  - Various animal packs
- **Tools Used:** Unity Editor, ProBuilder, TextMesh Pro
